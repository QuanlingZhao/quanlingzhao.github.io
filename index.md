## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the **University of California San Diego**, advised by Prof. Tajana Rosing. My research develops **efficient and theoretically grounded machine learning methods**, with interests spanning randomized and kernel methods, efficient LLM/VLM inference, and vector-symbolic / hyperdimensional computing. I am particularly interested in methods that combine principled representations with practical efficiency and systems impact.

## Research Interests

- **Efficient machine learning:** efficient inference, representation learning, anomaly detection, and statistical learning.
- **LLM/VLM efficiency:** KV-cache reuse, quantization, and efficient inference for long-context and agentic systems.
- **Randomized and kernel methods:** scalable density estimation, random features, and kernel approximation.
- **Vector-symbolic / hyperdimensional computing:** efficient representations, learning, memory, and systems co-design.

## Selected Research

**Superposed Latent Autoencoder (SLAE).** A representation-compression approach that keeps individual latent representations wide and stores multiple latents together through learned superposition, improving the reconstruction--memory tradeoff under matched storage. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01158)

**RGLD: Randomized Global-Local Density Estimation.** An ultra-fast unsupervised tabular anomaly detector that combines randomized global density estimation with projected local-neighbor evidence across randomized views. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28970)

**Superposed Inference for Hyperdimensional Computing.** A multiplexed inference framework that encodes multiple samples in superposition and recovers per-sample predictions through structured demixing, targeting lower encoding and inference cost.

**AgentKVShift.** A training-free KV-cache reuse method for agentic memory systems that corrects reused KV states with probe-guided residual shifts, reducing recomputation while preserving answer quality. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21604)

**HDDB.** A hardware--software co-design for noise-robust in-storage SQL predicate processing on ferroelectric NAND using hyperdimensional representations, developed in collaboration with IBM. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18234)

**WaferHDC.** An online open-world framework for detecting previously unseen wafer defects and incrementally discovering recurring defect modes without defect labels or a predefined number of classes. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3840404.3848291)
